Our Solution

An intelligent system that instantly analyzes news content and determines authenticity using AI

🎯 Goal: Empower users to identify fake news in real-time with AI-backed explanations
  • Analyzes text content, URLs, and even images (via OCR)
  • Provides clear verdicts with confidence scores
  • Explains misleading elements in detail
  • Offers fact-checking verification for claims

Key Features

📝 Multi-Input Support

Analyze news from direct text, URLs, or image content with OCR technology

🤖 AI-Powered Analysis

✅ Confidence Scoring

Get reliability metrics to understand how confident the verdict is

💡 Detailed Explanations

Automated fact-checking breaks down individual claims within articles

Get instant results without waiting for manual review
